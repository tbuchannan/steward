# Post-MVP and Non-Goals

**Status:** Accepted
**Last verified:** 2026-07-30

## Candidate Post-MVP Features

- Account-to-account transfers
- CSV transaction imports
- Copying a prior month's budget
- Net-worth history
- Spending analytics and additional charts
- Investment holdings, pricing, and performance summaries
- Recurring-transaction detection
- Financial goals
- Password recovery and password changes
- Email verification and email changes
- Multiple-session management
- Additional display and financial preferences

Candidate features require their own product decision before implementation.

## Explicit MVP Non-Goals

### Financial integrations

- Bank or brokerage synchronization
- Plaid integration
- Automatic institutional imports
- Payment processing, money transfer, or bill payment
- Live investment pricing
- Cryptocurrency wallets

### Financial advice

- Tax preparation or calculations
- Credit-score monitoring
- Automated trading
- AI-generated financial advice
- Debt-repayment recommendations
- Multi-currency conversion

### Identity and collaboration

- Social login, passkeys, or magic links
- Multi-factor authentication
- Household workspaces or shared budgets
- Invitations and workspace roles
- Enterprise identity providers

### Platforms and infrastructure

- Native mobile or desktop applications
- Browser extensions
- Offline synchronization
- Public third-party APIs
- Microservices, Kubernetes, or multi-region active-active systems
- Enterprise availability or compliance guarantees

New MVP scope must support the primary workflow, remain achievable for a solo developer, fit the demo, and justify its implementation and maintenance cost.
